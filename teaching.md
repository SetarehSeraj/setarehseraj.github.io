# Teaching

I have taught 50 sections of two core undergraduate courses at Baruch College's Zicklin School of Business since 2022, reaching roughly 1,400 students across in-person, online, and hybrid formats. My classes run on human connection, live problem-solving, games, and Harvard-style teaching cases rather than lectures, and every section is treated as its own small experiment. In 2026, this work received the Zicklin School of Business Dean's Teaching Award.
